## 10. Keel: verified in-kernel proof checker for proof-carrying BPF

**Source:** Cai, Chang, Nagarakatte, “Keel: A Verified In-Kernel Proof Checker for Proof Carrying BPF.” arXiv:2611.11117, 8 November 2026.

**Loop:** Existing BCF in-kernel checker contained 13 bugs (unsound rewrite rules, unchecked proof steps, memory-safety errors) that allowed forged certificates to accept unsafe eBPF programs. Keel specifies the checker as a pure functional program in Lean, proves soundness against bit-vector semantics, decouples certificate steps from rewrite rules, implements it in a deeply embedded C fragment (EmbC) with a refinement proof free of undefined behavior and memory leaks, and establishes linear time and memory bounds by amortized potential. Measured 1.3× faster overall and 2.0× faster on the slowest 1.4 % of certificates.

**mbcad reading:** Verified residual control of a security-critical checker. Soundness and resource bounds are established outside the kernel; the rewrite rules can be extended without altering the checker. No shared adjacency with the energy sheet.

**Status:** formal-methods neighbor (Lean specification + EmbC refinement). Not a production in the auditor. Does not write \(\dot{E}\). Does not become a face. Schema remains `mbcad.receipt.v0`.
