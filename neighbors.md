# Neighbor patterns (schema-adjacent)

These are empirical neighbors. They are not core laws, not faces, and not isomorphisms.
They do not write \(\dot E\). Schema id remains `mbcad.receipt.v0` on the kernel receipt.
Named tokens remain commentary only.

## 1. Categorization-density-Merge under HSD
Source: Benítez-Burraco & Hoshi (v7uya).  
Loop: amodal categorization → HSD-enabled cross-modality → critical token density → productive Merge → further categorization.  
mbcad reading: hysteresis gate (HSD as participation threshold) that allows a load face (token density) to enable a builder.  
Status: empirical neighbor. Not a production in the auditor.

## 2. Narrative packing under the same HSD gate
Source: Benítez-Burraco & Ferretti (u5adx).  
Loop: proto-narrative → HSD-selected narrative-brain hardware + language complexification → inner narrative / shared storytelling → further prosociality.  
mbcad reading: higher-order pack/readout that integrates faces (events, characters, temporal/causal links) and remains return-sufficient even without externalized speech.  
Status: empirical neighbor. Not a production in the auditor.

## 3. Color–emotion association with positivity / homogenization offset
Source: Xu & Benítez-Burraco (osf.io/pnc3q_v2).  
Loop: color terms × emotion terms linked by metaphor/metonymy; humans maintain cross-linguistic variance (Spanish vs. Chinese); GPT-4o tracks the average pattern but applies a positivity bias and reduces cross-linguistic intensity differences.  
mbcad reading: minimal blending case. Provenance-sensitive load (language-specific intensity) versus a gated statistical readout that is return-sufficient for the mean yet fail-closed on marked variance.  
Status: empirical neighbor. Not a production in the auditor.

## 4. Absence of cross-linguistic phonosymbolism (sr- reduplications)
Source: Benítez-Burraco, Xu & Lisowska (osf.io/8pdxq_v1).  
Result: association between sr- onset and contempt appears only among Polish speakers and is stronger for real words than pseudowords; not shared by other Indo-European or non-Indo-European groups.  
mbcad reading: negative case. Candidate universal/iconic packing fails to close outside the language-specific lexical source. Gate is lexical and provenance-sensitive; unmarked center remains unmarked.  
Status: empirical neighbor (negative). Not a production in the auditor.

## 5. Language as both proxy and trigger of behavioral modernity
Source: Benítez-Burraco (ScienceDirect S2950236526000277 / PsyArXiv a42de_v1).  
Loop: complex language (rich vocabulary, enhanced categorization, sophisticated syntax) both reflects and enables further social, technological, and symbolic complexification under HSD-mediated prosociality.  
mbcad reading: higher-order pack that integrates token inventory, categorization operator, and builder; once density and participation are sufficient, it loads further faces that feed back into the language face. Proxy reading is passive readout; trigger reading is active operator under the same gated regime.  
Status: empirical neighbor. Not a production in the auditor.

All five stay marked as neighbors. None is imported into the stepper. None claims a universal physical law or an interdimensional translation. Negative cases prevent over-extension of the unmarked center.
